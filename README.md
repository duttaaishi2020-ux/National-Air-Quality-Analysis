# National Air Quality Analysis

Analysis of daily air-quality readings for 10 Indian cities, 2015–2016: which cities have the worst air, when pollution peaks, which pollutants drive the Air Quality Index (AQI), and what happened during Delhi's November 2016 smog.

**Dataset:** [Air Quality Data in India](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/rohanrao/air-quality-data-in-india) (CPCB data, compiled on Kaggle), trimmed to 2015–2016.

## Key questions

1. Which cities have the worst air, both on average and by number of "Poor or worse" days?
2. Did air quality change from 2015 to 2016, city by city?
3. Which months and seasons are worst, and how big is the seasonal swing?
4. Which pollutants move most closely with AQI?
5. How bad was Delhi's November 2016 smog compared with the same days in 2015?
6. How reliable is the data (coverage by city and pollutant)?
